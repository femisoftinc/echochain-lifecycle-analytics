# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Name
EchoChain – Circular Economy & Secondary Market Lifecycle Analytics

## Business Problem

Manufacturers track products only until they are sold.
After the sale, companies lose visibility into the product lifecycle.

Because of this, companies do not know:

- Which products have high resale value.
- Which components fail most often.
- Which products should be refurbished.
- Which products should be included in buy-back programs.

## Business Objective

Build a data analytics solution that combines:

1. Product Manufacturing Data (BOM)
2. Warranty / Failure Data
3. Secondary Market Listings

to help companies understand the complete product lifecycle.

## Expected Dashboard

The dashboard should show:

- Circularity Score
- Products with highest resale value
- Most frequently failed components
- Average resale price
- Product lifecycle insights
